05 / Teacher guide & integrity

Teacher Hub

A concise planning and evidence guide for the learning resource.

For educators and assessors

Curriculum alignment

KSSR Mathematics Year 4 — Domain: Geometry; Content Standard 6.3 Perimeter and Area.

  • 6.3.1: Determine the perimeter of polygons with up to eight sides.
  • 6.3.2: Determine the area of specified shapes using unit squares and formulae.
  • 6.5.1: Solve problems involving space (supplementary application).
The resource deliberately focuses on squares and rectangles within these standards. It does not claim complete coverage of all shapes listed in the DSKP.

Misconception-to-intervention map

  • Perimeter vs area: Highlight the outline versus fill the interior with unit squares.
  • Equal perimeter → equal area: Construct 2 × 8 and 5 × 5 with perimeter 20 and areas 16 and 25.
  • Equal area → equal perimeter: Construct 3 × 8 and 4 × 6 with area 24 and perimeters 22 and 20.
  • Incorrect units: Compare length units with square units.

Facilitation sequence

  1. Diagnose: Invite explanations before correction.
  2. Predict: Ask what might change when dimensions move.
  3. Explore: Let pupils alter variables independently.
  4. Conjecture: Require a statement and evidence.
  5. Challenge: Find counterexamples to general claims.
  6. Apply: Design a garden using limited fencing.
  7. Reflect: Compare initial and current explanations.

Responsible AI use

AI assists the developer in drafting differentiated hints, examples and misconception supports. All items require mathematical verification. Pupils should reason before accessing a hint.

Important: The pre-written guidance cards remain available offline. The optional Live AI Tutor requests new hints through a protected Netlify serverless function that calls Google Gemini when configured. Automatic assessment remains deterministic and does not depend on AI.

No pupil names, login details or identifying data are collected by this website. The optional AI Tutor transmits only the selected topic, hint level and rectangle dimensions to Google after explicit user consent; see the AI Integration & Privacy Guide.
